Karma Rules & FAQs

Why are coupons priced at 1,500+ points?

We partner directly with tier-1 brands (Apple, Amazon, Starbucks, Sony). Pricing coupons at 1,500+ ensures substantial real-world savings (like ₹500 - ₹3,000 cash equivalent) rather than micro discounts.

How do I redeem and use my coupon code?

Click "Redeem Now". A unique digital voucher code will be generated immediately. Apply it at checkout in the partner app (e.g. Zomato, Starbucks, Apple, or Uber).

Do Karma points or redeemed codes expire?

Karma points remain valid for 12 months. Once a voucher is redeemed, promo codes typically carry a 90-day redemption window.
