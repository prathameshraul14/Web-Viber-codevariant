Blind Escrow Protocol

Why use Escrow Handovers?

Meeting up with strangers to return a lost wallet or phone can be unsafe or inconvenient. Our Secure Hubs ensure 100% privacy. Finders drop the item off, owners pick it up on their own schedule, and nobody has to exchange phone numbers or meet in person.

Step-by-Step Security

How It Works in 3 Steps

1

Generate QR Code

The finder selects any Secure Hub on the map and generates a tamper-evident Drop-Off QR token.

2

Secure Drop-Off

Hand the item to the barista, teller, or locker bay. They scan the code to log it into escrow storage.

3

Owner Retrieves

The verified owner verifies the blind secret, receives an unlock token, and retrieves their item safely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long will a Secure Hub hold my item?

Most hubs will securely hold your item for up to 72 hours. After that period, unclaimed items are transferred to designated municipal lost and found archives according to local protocols.

Is there a fee to use a Secure Hub?

No! Using FindIt Escrow Handovers is 100% free for both the finder and the owner. We partner with local businesses and transit agencies.

How do hubs verify the item is mine?

When an item is dropped off, it is logged into the system. You must complete the in-app "Zero-Knowledge" verification challenge to generate your unique Pick-Up QR code, which hub staff scan before release.

What if I can't pick it up in time?

You can request a 24-hour extension directly within the app, or authorize a verified friend or family member to pick it up on your behalf by transferring the QR code.
